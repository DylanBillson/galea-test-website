What the Galea platform does

Structured tools that help general healthcare professionals guide conversations, triage needs, and refer with clarity.

Triage & guide patients

Discreet, guided self-assessments help patients reflect — while giving clinicians early insight.

  • Earlier signals without longer appointments
  • Clearer next-step recommendations
  • Supports safer prioritisation

Document & refer consistently

Capture concerns in a consistent format and generate summaries that support defensible referrals.

  • Less variation between clinicians
  • Stronger referral quality
  • Better continuity of care

Support engagement over time

Keep patients engaged even when they won’t access specialist services — with patient-controlled follow-ups.

  • Optional check-ins
  • Outcome visibility
  • Patient control over sharing

This system works in primary care clinics, OH departments, A&Es, and emergency response environments.

Why it matters

You’re often the first contact for people struggling with mental health — and sometimes the last. Galea helps you:

  • Spot issues early — before they escalate
  • Prioritise who needs more urgent care
  • Improve documentation and referrals
  • Reduce appointment burden by guiding self-reflection
  • Stay connected to patients after the appointment
  • Protect time and reduce burnout — especially when mental health isn’t your specialty

Built for speed and safety

Quick check-ins and clear summaries help you move from “something feels off” to a safer, clearer next step — without turning every appointment into a mental health assessment.
