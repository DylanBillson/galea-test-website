What the Galea platform does

Digital tools for counsellors in schools, universities, and workplaces — designed to support your judgement and reduce admin.

Offer discreet check-ins

Give people a private, self-guided way to reflect — without forcing them to “perform” in the room.

  • Low-friction first step
  • Reduces stigma
  • Supports help-seeking

Structure conversations

Use guided prompts and clinically informed insights to make sessions clearer, safer, and more consistent.

  • Better focus
  • More consistent notes
  • Clearer next steps

Track change over time

Track progress or warning signs while protecting privacy — supporting continuity even when access is limited.

  • Optional follow-ups
  • Outcome visibility
  • Consent-based sharing

Why it matters

You are often the first or only support person available. Galea helps you:

  • Identify silent struggles early
  • Structure and document sessions efficiently
  • Provide a helpful starting point for someone reluctant to talk
  • Offer support to those waiting for more formal care
  • Stay connected to outcomes even across a large caseload

It’s built to enhance your skillset, not replace it — flexible, empathetic, and designed for your role.

Two-way learning

  • Self-assessments inform support sessions
  • Counsellor input improves future tool use
  • Aggregated trends inform your wellbeing strategy

Scale support with care

Built to support larger caseloads without losing empathy — helping you focus time where it matters most.

Benefits

Clear benefits for both counsellors and the people they support.

For counsellors

  • Gain insight before the conversation starts
  • Reduce the emotional labour of first sessions
  • Offer continuity between sessions — even remotely
  • Spot patterns and relapse risk early
  • Use adaptable language and levels — suitable for young people or adults

For students / employees

  • Reduce stigma with private self-checks
  • Build confidence to speak up
  • Track progress on their terms
  • Feel heard, supported, and better prepared for conversations
  • Access tools even if sessions are infrequent or unavailable

Flexible use for large-scale support

White-labelling and organisational dashboards available.

University & workplace benefit

When students or employees opt into enhanced support, your institution receives platform credits — helping you do more, for less.
