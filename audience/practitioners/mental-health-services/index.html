Why it matters to mental health teams

Whether you’re working in NHS services, charity-based support, or private clinics, you’re facing increasing complexity and demand. This platform helps by:

  • Prioritising urgency — triage based on real mental health need
  • Improving accuracy and consistency — even across teams
  • Speeding up assessments — structured forms reduce documentation time
  • Enhancing clinical confidence — with machine learning-driven benchmarking that flags anomalies and suggests norms
  • Supporting safer decisions — grounded in data and a validated clinical model
  • Reducing gaps between sessions — with optional patient-led check-ins
  • Improving over time — the more it’s used, the smarter and more refined it becomes

All of this contributes to better care, clearer communication, and more resilient services.

Clinical judgement stays central

Galea is designed to support professional decision-making — not replace it. Teams stay in control, with clearer structure and better insight.
