Where this fits in the care & research pathway

The platform operates across real-world health and social care environments — enabling studies that reflect real delivery contexts, not just ideal conditions.

  • Before care: Individuals engage privately and may opt into pre-screening or observational research
  • During care: Clinician-led assessments support both service delivery and structured research protocols
  • After care: Follow-up and outcome linkage support continuity and post-intervention insights

The platform can support integration with external datasets (EHRs, service outcomes, clinical coding) using privacy-respecting identifiers — improving data quality, equity analysis, and population-level modelling.
