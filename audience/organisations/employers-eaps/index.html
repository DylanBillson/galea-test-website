The utilisation problem — and the opportunity

Despite good intentions, EAPs are vastly underused, even with proven benefits and ROI. The most common reasons are stigma, lack of awareness, and fear of confidentiality breaches.

How Galea bridges the gap

With self-guided check-ins, optional anonymity, and intelligent escalation tools, the platform encourages early engagement — especially from people who would never pick up the phone or talk to HR.

Designed for trust

  • Clear privacy boundaries

Where this fits in the employee journey

Support mental health from prevention to recovery — with clear pathways and better continuity.

  • Before intervention: private check-ins reduce stigma and raise early flags
  • During support: summaries and referrals streamline professional conversations
  • After support: optional follow-ups help maintain engagement and reduce relapse

Why it pays to invest

Mental health support isn’t just ethical — it’s strategic. Organisations that support mental health often see better retention, fewer absences, and higher productivity.

  • Deloitte found strong returns on workplace mental health investment (commonly cited as around £5 return for every £1).
  • Employees are more likely to stay with employers that take mental health seriously.
  • Earlier support reduces escalation and long-term absence risk.
