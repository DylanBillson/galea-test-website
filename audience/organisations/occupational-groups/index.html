Built for high-stress roles

Whether you’re supporting firefighters, police officers, paramedics, NHS frontline teams, farmers, teachers, care workers, or other high-pressure sectors, Galea helps people reflect and take action — in a way that feels familiar, private, and effective.

Private self-checks

Help workers assess their mental health discreetly — without needing to “explain it” first.

  • Mobile-first access
  • Trauma-aware language
  • Works around shifts

Clear signposting

Support workers to find the right next step — peer networks, OH, union routes, or external services.

  • Low-friction engagement
  • Respectful pathways
  • Better follow-through

Early intervention insight

Track patterns at group level to spot hotspots and needs early — while protecting individuals.

  • Post-incident trends
  • Role-based risk patterns

Where this fits in the care pathway

This platform supports mental health across your sector or workforce — before, during, and after support. It’s easy to deploy across mobile-first, shift-based, or geographically dispersed groups.

  • Before support: workers reflect privately or are encouraged to engage by a supervisor, union rep, or peer
  • During support: support teams use guided tools to structure safe, useful conversations
  • After support: optional check-ins support relapse prevention and follow-up after critical events

Deployment options

  • Union-led, employer-funded, or hybrid models
  • Co-branded portals for associations and member groups
  • Role-based access for OH, peer support, or partners
  • Anonymous or named reporting based on permissions
