Where this fits in the care pathway

Galea enhances every stage of the mental health journey — from waiting list insight to recovery tracking. Insights can come from clinical assessments, individual self-check-ins, or both.

  • Pre-contact: individuals assess privately while waiting — enabling smarter triage
  • During care: practitioners use structured tools to support risk identification and documentation
  • Post-assessment: monitoring and population insight support recovery, safety, and service planning

When integrated with EPR/EHR systems (using privacy-respecting identifiers), the platform can link assessment and monitoring data to outcomes — improving longitudinal tracking, evaluation, and commissioning decisions.
