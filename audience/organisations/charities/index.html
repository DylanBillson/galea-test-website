Where this fits in the support pathway

The platform is designed to be flexible across diverse support models — and easy to integrate into your delivery.

  • Before engagement: people can self-assess and begin reflecting independently
  • During support: counsellors or key workers can use guided tools to structure sessions or inform decisions
  • After contact: monitoring tools help you track wellbeing across groups, teams, or whole communities
