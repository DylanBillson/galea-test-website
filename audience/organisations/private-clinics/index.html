What the platform does

Galea provides a clinical-grade assessment and monitoring system that supports high-quality care — without taking away your clinic’s identity or the therapeutic relationship.

Before appointments

Invite clients to complete structured check-ins ahead of sessions, so you can spend more time on care and less on discovery.

  • Pre-session insight
  • Clear themes and prompts
  • Reduces intake friction

During care

Use guided, risk-aware tools to support consistent documentation and safer decision-making across clinicians.

  • Structured assessments
  • Risk-aware flags
  • Clinic-wide consistency

Between sessions

Optional client-led monitoring helps track progress over time and supports continuity between appointments.

  • Progress tracking
  • Early relapse signals
  • Client-controlled sharing

Why it matters

Private clinics thrive on personalised care and client satisfaction — but also need efficient workflows, defensible documentation, and a consistent standard of care as teams grow.

  • Efficient clinical workflows without cutting corners
  • Risk-aware documentation and clearer escalation
  • Consistency across practitioners, locations, and service lines
  • Scalable models for growth without compromising quality

Galea supports all of this while keeping your clinic’s identity front and centre.
