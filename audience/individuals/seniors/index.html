Growing older comes with its own challenges

Life experience brings strength — but also stress, loss, uncertainty, or change. It’s okay to feel overwhelmed, sad, irritable, or stuck. It doesn’t mean something is wrong with you. It means you’re human.

Whether you’re dealing with grief, isolation, health changes, or simply wondering why you don’t feel like yourself anymore — we’re here to help you explore it safely.
