About us

Our mission, vision & values

What guides how we build Galea, make decisions, and work with the people and organisations we support.

Our mission

  • To transform how mental health concerns are identified and addressed
  • To provide instant mental health expertise and advice
  • To improve access to mental health services

Our vision

  • No barriers to effective mental health services.
  • Putting people at the heart of their mental health care.
  • Discussions that focus on what matters in a truly collaborative and equal partnership.

Our values

  • Everybody has an equal right to understanding and improving their mental health.
  • Any technology used to assess people's mental health must have outputs that are fully interpretable and can be validated by those people.
  • All technologies relating to people's mental health must support equality of access and decision making.
