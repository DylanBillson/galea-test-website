Data security & privacy

Trust is earned. We treat mental health data with confidentiality, control, and care.

Protecting what matters most

At Galea, we understand that mental health data is deeply personal and that trust must be earned. That’s why data security and privacy aren’t afterthoughts. They’re core to everything we do.

From day one, we’ve designed Galea to prioritise confidentiality, control, and ethical responsibility. Whether you’re an individual exploring your concerns or an organisation supporting others, you can be confident that your information is handled with care.

Our approach to data security is shaped by industry standards, robust encryption, and a commitment to transparency. We’re continuously evolving our systems to stay at the forefront of best practice, not just meeting requirements.

  • We don’t treat data as just another asset
