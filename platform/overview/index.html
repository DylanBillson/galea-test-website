Self-assessment

You don’t need to be in crisis to check in on your mental health.

Whether you’re feeling low, anxious, overwhelmed, or just not yourself — this is a simple, private way to understand what might be going on. It’s a first step toward clarity, confidence, and (if needed) connection to further support.

  • Make sense of how you’re feeling
  • Spot early signs of concern
  • Explore what support might be right for you

How it works

Clinically validated frameworks, simplified into human language — as a chat-style experience with Nomi, or a structured assessment.

  • No data shared without explicit consent
  • No pressure — just clarity and direction

Who it’s for

For anyone wondering if what they’re feeling is worth exploring further — including high-pressure and underserved groups.

What it’s not

Not a diagnosis and not a replacement for professional advice — a starting point for understanding and next steps.

Enterprise reporting

Unlock meaningful mental health data across your service or organisation — to understand needs, manage risk, and drive better outcomes.

Galea helps you:

  • Identify unmet needs across populations
  • Monitor trends over time
  • Evaluate programme impact and risk exposure
  • Support governance, compliance, and funding cases

Designed for systems thinking — whether you serve 100 people or 1 million.
