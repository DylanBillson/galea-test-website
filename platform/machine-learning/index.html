What makes Galea different — and why you can trust it

Galea isn’t just another chatbot or AI tool offering advice. It’s built on a completely different foundation: decades of expert knowledge and structured reasoning from thousands of qualified practitioners.

Where most AI systems rely purely on language patterns (like large language models or LLMs), Galea uses what’s called Cognitive AI — a form of machine learning designed to mirror how real practitioners think. It doesn’t guess based on internet data. Instead, it draws on a carefully structured model of mental health expertise, originally developed through interviews with over 3,000 professionals.

Galea’s technology has been refined over years of academic and clinical validation. It doesn’t just mimic understanding — it genuinely helps structure, assess, and support complex mental health decisions in a transparent, accountable way.

This means Galea’s insights are

  • Explainable – You can see how each conclusion was reached
  • Consistent – It doesn’t hallucinate or drift off-topic
  • Clinically grounded – Based on real-world mental health expertise, not crowd-sourced information
