Top questions

The most common things people ask when they first find Galea.

What is Galea? Overview

Galea is a digital mental-health decision support platform. It helps people, practitioners, and organisations understand concerns earlier, structure next steps, and generate clearer summaries for follow-on support.

How it works
Is Galea a diagnosis? Clarity

No — Galea is designed to support understanding and decision-making, not replace clinical judgement. It can help structure concerns and support safer next steps, but it’s not a standalone diagnosis.

Who is Galea for? Audience

Galea supports individuals who want clarity, practitioners who need structured tools, and organisations who need population-level insight and reporting. Each audience has tailored features and workflows.

Individuals Practitioners Organisations
Can I share results with a GP or therapist? Reports

Yes — where enabled, Galea can generate clear summaries that help you explain what’s been going on and support next-step conversations. Sharing is consent-based and controlled by the person completing the assessment.

Privacy & data

Straight answers about confidentiality, security, and consent.

How do you handle data privacy and GDPR? Privacy

We design around privacy-by-default principles and UK GDPR requirements. We aim to collect only what’s needed, protect it with appropriate safeguards, and keep sharing consent-based.

Data security
Is it confidential? Safety

We treat mental health information as sensitive. Access controls, encrypted storage, and audit-friendly processes support confidentiality. What’s shared (and with whom) depends on the pathway and consent settings in use.

Do you sell personal data? Trust

The intent of the platform is support and insight — not data brokerage. If you need a formal statement for procurement or governance, contact us and we can provide the right documentation for your context.
